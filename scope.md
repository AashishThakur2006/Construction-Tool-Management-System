# Construction Tool & Logistics Management System - Project Scope

## Project Overview
A web-based application designed to help construction companies efficiently manage
projects, equipment, a tool inventory, material logistics, employees, maintenance
and payments through a centralized platform.

## In Scope
- User registration and login (Supabase Auth)
- Dashboard with cross-module statistics
- Project management
- Equipment / machine management and allocation to projects
- Machine transfer log between sites
- Tool & inventory management with quantity tracking
- Tool check-out / check-in (issue / return) with a movement ledger
- Material delivery tracking (inbound / outbound, status flow)
- Employee management
- Maintenance request management
- Payment management
- Reporting across finance, equipment, maintenance and logistics
- Search and filter functionality
- Role-based access for Owner and Site Engineer

## Out of Scope
- GPS tracking
- IoT-enabled machine monitoring
- Mobile application
- AI-based maintenance prediction
- Third-party accounting software integration

## Target Users
- Owner
- Site Engineer

## Expected Outcome
A centralized system that reduces paperwork, improves resource and inventory
tracking, and simplifies construction and logistics operations.
