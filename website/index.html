DATUM +2.5 M · FIRST LIFT · MANIFESTO

A building should tell you how it stands up the moment you walk in.

Most construction hides its work. Steel disappears behind plasterboard; loads are carried in secret; the ceiling is a lie about the roof. We refuse that. At Terrane, the structure is the finish: the beam you see is the beam that holds you, the concrete keeps the grain of the boards that formed it, and every joint is cut to be looked at.

We are architects and builders in one room — the drawings and the crews share an address — so nothing is designed that we do not know, in our hands, how to make.
